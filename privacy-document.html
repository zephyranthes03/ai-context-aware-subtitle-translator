Privacy Policy — Context-Aware Document Translator

Last updated: 2026-09-03

This extension does not operate a server, and the developer does not collect, receive, or store any of your data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or telemetry of any kind in the extension.

Everything it does happens either locally in your browser, or as a direct connection from your browser to a service you chose and configured.

What leaves your browser, and to where

1. The AI connection you configured

To translate, the extension sends the text of the page or the text you selected to the model connection you set up in Settings. That connection is one of:

  • a model running on your own machine (for example a local server on 127.0.0.1) — in this case the text never leaves your computer;
  • an API endpoint you supply a key for; or
  • a subscription CLI bridge running on your own machine, which relays to the AI subscription you already pay for.

The extension has no endpoint of its own and no fallback destination. If you have not configured a connection, no translation request is made at all.

Along with the text, a request may carry translation context you created: the renderings you pinned for the work you are reading, and the target language.

2. The website you are already reading

Two features fetch a page from the site you are on, using your own browser session, exactly as opening the page yourself would:

  • Next-page pre-translation, when you enable it, fetches the following page so it is ready when you get there.
  • Fetch work details, which reads the work's own page for its title and description. This happens only when you pin your first rendering for a work, or when you press the button for it — never on opening a chapter, and once per work.

These requests go to that site and nowhere else.

3. An AI website, only when you send something to it

“Ask on AI site” opens a new tab at the AI website you configured and fills in the question. Nothing is submitted until you send it there yourself, and you can edit or delete anything in it first.

4. A problem report, only when you submit one

The “Report a problem” link opens a pre-filled form. It carries the extension version, your target language, the kind of connection you use and its model name, and your interface language. The address of the page you are on is included only if you tick the box for it, which is off by default.

Nothing is transmitted by opening the form. Every line is visible and editable, and you can delete any of it before pressing Submit. The form is a Google Form; Google’s privacy policy applies to what you submit there.

What is stored, and where

All of it is stored locally in your browser, using Chrome’s extension storage:

  • your settings (connections, target language, display preferences);
  • the renderings you pinned, per work and per target language;
  • recently translated pages, so going back does not re-translate — this is session storage and is discarded when the browser closes.

None of it is transmitted anywhere. You can delete all of it by removing the extension, and individual works from the Series knowledge page.

Site access

The extension requests access to a site only when you enable it there, or for the tab you are on when you ask it to translate. It does not read pages in the background and does not run on sites you have not activated it on.

Sensitive information

Because you choose the connection, you decide where your text goes. If you point the extension at a model running on your own machine, the text being translated does not leave that machine. If you point it at a third-party API or subscription, that provider’s terms and privacy policy apply to what is sent.

Contact

Problems and questions: the “Report a problem” link in the extension, or the issue tracker.
